Siteplan

Site Name

Big Mini's Quizatron:
I chose to name the website "Big Mini's Quizatron" because "Big Mini" is what I call my rabbit named Mini, and the quiz is going to be the highlight of the site.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the site is to provide information on the breeds of rabbits that make up Mini's mix and to provide a fun quiz to test visitors' knowledge of rabbit information found on the site. The home page will be all about Mini and the other pages will provide more detailed information about each breed. There will also be a seperate page for the quiz.

Scenarios

Scenario 1: A visitor wants to learn more about the breeds of rabbits that make up Mini's mix.

Scenario 2: A visitor wants to test their knowledge of rabbit information found on the site.

Color Scheme

The color scheme for the site will be a combination of soft pastel colors, such as light pink, light blue, and light green, to show a gentle and playful vibe. Rabbits reflect this nature. These are also Easter related colors and most people associate Easter with rabbits.

#a0e6b2 - leafy green for the header and footer

#efe1c8 - soft hay color for the main background

#fad3cf - soft rose/peach color & #b8e0f3 - sky blue for sections, headings, smaller backgrounds

#2D2D2D - dark gray & #3E2723 - dark brown for text

Typography

For headers and titles I like to use the "Ubuntu" font, it's really easy to read and has a sort of friendly appearance. For the main content, I use an Arial font.
