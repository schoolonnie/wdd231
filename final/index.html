About Mini

Mini is a unique rabbit with a mix of Flemish Giant, Continental Giant, and Rex breeds. Her actual name is Minseo (민서), which is Korean. I call her "Mini" because it's easier for most people to say and is ironic due to her massive size. She is very individualistic and has a strong personality. She can be territorial at times, but is also sweet and playful. She loves to chew on applewood and bamboo sticks. Flemish Giants and Continental Giants are known for their very large size and gentle nature. Rex rabbits are known for their distinctive fur and playful demeanor.
